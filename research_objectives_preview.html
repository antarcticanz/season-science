K020A

Virus Dispersal in the Antarctic Cryosphere

Overview

This project investigates how viruses disperse across Antarctic lake and soil systems, with a focus on the role of wind-driven transport between isolated basins.

Objectives

  • Quantify airborne viral abundance above ice-covered lakes
  • Compare viral communities across three geographically separated sites
  • Test whether dispersal is limited by distance or by local environmental filtering

Methods

  1. Aerosol sampling using high-volume filters deployed for 72-hour intervals
  2. Metagenomic sequencing of collected biomass
  3. Bioinformatic comparison against reference viral genomes
